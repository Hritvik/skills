# Transport Comparison

Compare complete journeys, not headline fares. Verify dates, traveller count, baggage, taxes, transfers, tolls, fuel, rental conditions, layovers, arrival/departure time, cancellation terms, and usable vacation hours where relevant.

For flights, check transit/visa implications and airport transfers. For road trips, compare route families by road quality, seasonal hazards, traffic escape, stops, tolls, fuel, night-driving risk, and destination base. Vehicle ownership permits distance as a controlled compromise but does not erase road-safety or fatigue costs.

Lead with one practical option and up to two alternatives. Stop before selecting, holding, or booking transport.
