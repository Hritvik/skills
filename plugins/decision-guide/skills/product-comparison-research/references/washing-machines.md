# Washing-machine comparison guidance

## Separate comparison classes

Do not directly rank semi-automatic, fully automatic top-load, and front-load machines. Also separate materially different capacities when household needs make them non-substitutable.

## Establish suitability

Confirm household size, preferred capacity, dimensions, lid clearance, water availability and pressure, drainage, hard-water conditions, heater need, noise sensitivity, installation, and local serviceability.

## Hard gates

Exclude or flag machines with insufficient capacity, unclear model identity, unavailable delivery, unacceptable seller status, ambiguous warranty, incompatible installation, or unconditional final price above budget.

## Default scoring when reliability leads

- reliability and build quality: 25
- wash and rinse performance: 20
- service and comprehensive warranty: 15
- review evidence quality: 15
- value at current price: 10
- ownership cost: 5
- noise and usability: 5
- relevant features: 5

Change weights for the buyer and disclose the change. A serious recurring defect, unsafe behavior, or unusable local service is a disqualifier rather than a score penalty.

## Evidence to collect

- exact model number, year, capacity, dimensions, motor, drum, spin speed, energy label, water requirements, programs, and heater
- comprehensive versus component warranty, exclusions, installation terms, and required accessories
- wash, rinse, spin, noise, vibration, lint, rust, lid/control durability, drainage, and hard-water evidence
- service coverage, installation complaints, parts availability, repair patterns, and post-warranty risk
- unconditional price, conditional offers, fees, historical low, typical price, and price trajectory

## Review analysis

Separate product, seller, delivery, installation, and service complaints. Give more weight to exact-model verified purchases and reviews describing months of ownership. Detect mixed-variant pages, review bursts, duplicated language, incentives, and ratings dominated by delivery impressions.

Do not infer reliability from motor-warranty length, star rating, or review volume alone. Report recurring defect themes even when the average rating remains high.
