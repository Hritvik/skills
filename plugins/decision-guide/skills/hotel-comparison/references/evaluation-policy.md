# Hotel evaluation policy

## Eligibility gates

A candidate is eligible only when the direct listing retains the exact dates and guest composition and provides a valid room allocation. Verify maximum occupancy rather than assuming an extra adult can use an existing bed. Treat a mattress, sofa bed, or futon as confirmed only when the room rules allow it and any charge is known.

Budget means the complete stay. Add displayed taxes and mandatory charges; identify breakfast, resort fees, extra-person charges, cleaning fees, and payment conditions separately. Never call a price final when compulsory fees remain unknown.

Inspect recent bedroom and bathroom photographs for every finalist, plus exterior and common spaces when atmosphere matters. Reject or downgrade visible dampness, damaged surfaces, stained linen, poor bathroom maintenance, misleading cropping, or a material mismatch between room types shown and rooms offered.

## Hard rejection rules

Use **Hard rejection** outside weighted scoring for:

- unavailable or date/occupancy-mismatched inventory;
- total known cost above a hard budget;
- unconfirmed sleeping capacity;
- repeated recent reviews reporting serious hygiene, pests, mould, unsafe access, or booking refusal;
- materially misleading room identity or photographs;
- a hard accessibility, location, safety, or property-type mismatch.

One isolated complaint is not automatically a rejection. Consider recency, detail, corroboration, host response, and cross-platform agreement.

## Default ranking weights

Use these only when the trip brief does not imply different priorities:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Bedroom and bathroom quality | 22 |
| Recent reviews | 20 |
| Fit with stated preferences | 18 |
| Cleanliness and maintenance evidence | 15 |
| Total value | 10 |
| Location and accessibility | 7 |
| Booking flexibility | 5 |
| Evidence confidence | 3 |

Keep evidence confidence visible even when it is scored: a five-review property should not be presented with the certainty of a consistently reviewed property. When a requested experience—such as a coffee estate—is central, require direct evidence of that setting and increase preference-fit weight rather than treating a greenery view as equivalent.

## Distance expansion when the traveller has a car

When the traveller has a private vehicle and the nearby inventory cannot satisfy the hard budget and quality gates, expand geography before weakening the requested experience, bedroom or bathroom quality, review quality, sleeping capacity, or complete-stay budget.

Use practical driving time rather than straight-line distance. Start with roughly 60 minutes from the trip's anchor location, then expand to 75 minutes; consider up to 90 minutes only when the property is meaningfully better or is itself part of the trip experience. Account for mountain roads, monsoon conditions, night driving, parking, approach-road quality, and limited dining near remote properties.

For multi-centre destinations, search named regional belts and nearby towns rather than only increasing a radius around the best-known town. Re-rank expanded candidates using total itinerary friction: expected daily driving, attraction locations, meal availability, road confidence, and the value of the stay experience. Clearly state the driving-time trade-off.

Do not treat vehicle access as permission to ignore an explicit hard location boundary. Keep it trip-specific unless the user deliberately saves it as a durable profile preference.

## Source coverage and availability recovery

Do not conclude that a destination has no suitable inventory after checking only one or two large OTAs. When results remain weak, cover the source types that are relevant to the market:

- major hotel OTAs for live room/rate inventory;
- vacation-rental platforms for entire homes and independent hosts;
- metasearch or map listings for discovering alternate suppliers and official sites;
- official property booking engines for direct inventory;
- review platforms for recent guest photographs and cross-platform contradictions.

Prioritise sources that can retain the exact dates and guest composition in the listing URL or visible search state. Record which source actually proved availability; a search result, cached price, review page, or undated property page is discovery evidence only.

Treat MakeMyTrip and Goibibo, Expedia-group brands, Booking Holdings brands, or other shared/syndicated inventories as correlated unless the room allocation or availability is demonstrably different. Two correlated pages do not equal two independent confirmations.

For an otherwise excellent property that is unavailable on one source, perform an availability-recovery pass:

1. Check at least one independent OTA or rental platform where the property is listed.
2. Check the official website or official booking engine when identifiable.
3. Look for a different valid room combination for the same guests without changing dates or hiding extra-person charges.
4. Compare cancellation, payment, and total-price terms across recovered offers.
5. If only an official phone, email, WhatsApp, social account, or enquiry form can confirm inventory, label the property **direct confirmation required** rather than available.

Never call, message, submit an enquiry, log in, reserve, or hold inventory without explicit user authorisation. Do not use an unverified social profile or aggregator phone number as an official contact channel.

Before calling a broader search complete, report source coverage, recovered inventory, still-unavailable high-quality candidates, and any material coverage gaps caused by login walls, blocked pages, or missing exact-date controls.

## Contradiction checks

Before recommending, check:

- lifetime rating versus recent reviews;
- platform cleanliness score versus detailed hygiene reports;
- property marketing versus geolocated setting and exterior photographs;
- room photographs versus the exact offered room type;
- cancellation headline versus the selected rate;
- displayed total versus taxes and mandatory charges;
- map distance versus practical rainy-season access;
- one platform’s inventory or score versus cross-platform evidence.

Prefer the safer conclusion when important evidence conflicts, and explain the conflict.
