# Review analysis

Prioritize verified stays from the latest 12 months, then expand only when the sample is too small or a long-term pattern matters. Capture review date, stay date when available, room type, party type, score, positives, negatives, and platform.

Group comments into decision-relevant themes:

- bedroom, linen, bathroom, hot water, dampness, pests, and housekeeping;
- host responsiveness, booking acceptance, food consistency, and unexpected charges;
- noise, Wi-Fi, power, parking, approach road, weather exposure, and distance;
- whether photographs and descriptions matched the stay.

Repeated specific complaints outweigh generic praise. Detailed mixed reviews are often more useful than undifferentiated 10/10 ratings. Treat a small review count as low confidence even when the score is high. Check another platform for the leading candidates when a negative theme could reverse the recommendation.

Report a concise verdict—strong, generally good with caveats, mixed, concerning, or insufficient evidence—and name the themes that justify it. Do not average incompatible platform scores without explaining their different samples.
