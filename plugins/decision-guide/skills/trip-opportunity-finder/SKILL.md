# Trip Opportunity Finder

Screen broadly, then narrow cheaply. For international discovery, compare realistic primary transport, visa/entry cost and effort, minimum sensible duration, seasonal fit, daily ground cost, connectivity from the origin, and a contingency-aware all-in range.

Screen roughly 12–20 plausible destinations when the market supports it. Reject obvious budget, visa, season, safety, or duration mismatches before hotel-level research. Retain concise rejection reasons.

Return up to five meaningfully different opportunities: best overall, value, comfort/logistical ease, strongest experience match, and an optional stretch. Do not fill a slot with a weak or duplicate option. Mark all discovery-stage prices as estimates unless exact dated inventory was inspected.

Stop at an opportunity shortlist; itinerary construction belongs to `itinerary-builder`.
