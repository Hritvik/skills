## Question

What does an unspecified vacation budget include, and when is a trip safely within it?

## Resolution

Default to primary transport, visa, insurance, stay, local transport, food, activities, connectivity, mandatory fees, and contingency. Keep optional shopping separate. Comfortable feasibility requires expected cost at or below 90% of budget with the plausible upper range still inside budget.
